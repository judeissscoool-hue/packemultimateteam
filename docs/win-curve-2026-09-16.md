# Win projection correction

Status: implemented and tested locally, NOT LIVE. Automatic approval review
rejected the server deployment because the full request exceeded its
200,000-byte review limit. No production rules were registered and no site
release was pushed. Publish the server, register the new rules and allowlists,
then publish the site together after the deployment block is resolved. Do not
publish the frontend by itself.

The expanded roster's effective strengths were bunching at 78–4. The prior GMM
release calibrated only the 80–82 segment. Below it, each anchor interval used
its own cosine ease, repeatedly slowing progression near every anchor before
rounding to integer wins. This made 78 cover roughly effective 96.333–98.0,
while 79 covered only 98.0–99.0. It was a curve-shape issue, not an addition or
loss-count arithmetic error.

New runs use linear interpolation between the existing anchors up to effective
100 (80 wins). At 97.8, the result becomes 79–3 rather than 78–4. At 96.4 it
becomes 77–5 rather than 78–4. The 78 band becomes 96.5–97.75. No blanket boost
or new win randomness is introduced. All anchors and all 80–82 calculations
at effective 100 and above remain unchanged, including the exact floating-point
82 boundary near 102.4 and all-eight-cards-at-least-85 gate.

## Measurements

Re-scored 163,857 saved legal rosters through the actual browser and server
calculations, including the original 140,000-attempt GMM restoration samples
and two 20,000-attempt lower-strength Pack samples. Acquisition, pools, OVR,
chemistry, styles, legal swaps and optimisation are unchanged. This is a paired
re-scoring audit, not fresh simulation or a claim about live human win rates.

| Sample | Attempts | Completed rosters | 78 wins before → after | 82–0 before = after |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Modern Draft | 50,000 | 50,000 | 13,092 → 9,762 (26.184% → 19.524%) | 1,543 / 50,000 = 3.086% |
| Modern Pack | 50,000 | 38,437 | 9,133 → 6,797 | 1,285 / 50,000 = 2.570% |
| History Draft | 20,000 | 20,000 | 5,211 → 3,899 | 578 / 20,000 = 2.890% |
| History Pack | 20,000 | 15,421 | 3,686 → 2,725 | 541 / 20,000 = 2.705% |

The optimized Pack search omitted full rosters when an all-85+ lineup was
impossible; these attempts remain in the 82–0 denominator, but not in record
histograms. Supplementary gold-aware Pack samples cover 39,999 completed
lower-strength rosters (one incomplete attempt) and preserve every perfection
classification too. All sampled changes are -1, 0 or +1 wins; most are zero.
Across the entire mathematical domain, lower-strength curve segments can differ
by up to two wins. Wins plus losses always equals 82.

The prior accepted targets (~2.97% Draft and ~2.73% Pack) and their measured
restoration rates are preserved, not recalibrated. There is no acquisition
change requiring a new drafting policy; exact 82 classification is invariant
for every effective rating, not merely the sampled rosters.
